How it works

Move the marker to match how you feel. There’s no right answer.

Okay

You have energy, can focus, and enjoy spending time with others.

Tired

Focusing takes effort. You want to finish for the day, but still enjoy other things.

Overloaded

Small things irritate you. Tasks stay on your mind, and socializing feels like work.

Exhausted

Even things you enjoy feel draining. Small tasks take effort; you may want to be alone.

The descriptions are a rough guide, not a test or medical advice.
